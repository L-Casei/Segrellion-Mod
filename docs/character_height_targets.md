# Adult portrait height targets

The existing Segrellion heights are retained as authored.

Stormflame ordinary adult male targets average approximately 1.82 m:
Leo 1.86 m, Edwyn 1.82 m, Joffrey 1.81 m, Durran 1.83 m,
Donal 1.78 m, Donnel 1.82 m. Clifford is the exceptional giant,
with a target of 7 feet (2.1336 m); his giant trait is retained and
his DNA now uses giant_height. Eldon retains his dwarf DNA and trait.
These targets describe the authored portraits, not a dynasty-wide genetic rule.

Torgo Nudho and Missandei both target approximately 1.80 m as adults.
Their supplied genes are preserved except for gene_height. Their historical
birth dates are retained; Missandei is still a child in the 300 AC bookmark.
Hair overrides reproduce the supplied ruler designer selections for adults.

Height gene values are normalized bytes, not centimetres. Male and female
meshes and trait modifiers affect the result. These are approximate visual
settings; exact metre values and equal adult height require in-game comparison.
Leo, Edwyn and Donnel bookmark heights are synchronized with their DNA.

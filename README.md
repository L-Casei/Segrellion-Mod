# Segrellion Overhaul

Submod scaffold for a CK3 AGOT overhaul centered on House Segrellion.

Current state:

- Defines the mod descriptor.
- Adds a three-level special building chain: `Segrellion Fort` -> `Aeryn's Den` -> `Firstflame`.
- Assigns an empty special building slot to province `4427`, `b_berryport`, in `c_blackmark`.
- Requires the holder of `c_blackmark` to be `dynasty:dynn_Segrellion` to construct the chain.
- Adds a minimal `dynn_Segrellion` High Valyrian dynasty definition.
- Adds `house_Segrellion` for future characters.
- Adds `dynn_Vladwyn`, `house_Vladwyn`, and Tristan Vladwyn in Alex's court.
- Adds Alex, Alysanne, Daenerys, Aegor, Saera, Balerion, and Shiera Segrellion.
- Assigns `c_blackmark`/Vhalerion and `b_berryport`/Vhal to Alex on `8106.4.18`.
- Adds a playable Segrellion bookmark for `8106.4.18`.
- Renames `b_berryport` to Vhal and `c_blackmark` to Vhalerion.
- Uses AGOT's `building_special_lonely_light` mesh as a temporary map model.

Current target:

- Province: `4427`
- Barony: `b_berryport`
- County: `c_blackmark`
- Province history override: `history/provinces/00_k_dragonstone_prov.txt`

Planned overhaul scope:

- Segrellion playable bookmark.
- Segrellion characters and title history.
- Segrellion coat of arms and dynasty/house setup.
- Segrellion Valyrian steel sword.
- Plate of Segrell, a powerful Valyrian steel armor artifact.
- Final Firstflame 3D model, holding illustration, and icons.
